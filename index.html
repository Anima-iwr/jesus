Good to know

Questions are welcome.

Do I have to be Christian to join?

No. This is an explicitly Christian discipleship program centered on Jesus and the Bible. You are welcome if you already believe or are genuinely willing to explore Christian faith.

Is this really free?

Yes. There are no tuition, membership or participation fees. You are never expected to donate or purchase anything.

Who is the program for?

It is intended exclusively for adult women (18+), with a special welcome to university students, researchers and professionals around Heidelberg. Other adult women are welcome to inquire.

What is the time commitment?

The vision is a three-year journey, usually with two or three conversations each month, subject to mutual availability. You can stop participating at any time without pressure.

Are meetings online or in person?

Both are possible. In-person meetings are arranged by mutual agreement in appropriate public locations around Heidelberg; online conversations are also available.

Can I ask difficult questions or disagree?

Absolutely. Honest questions, thoughtful disagreement and respectful conversation are welcome. There is no requirement to claim a belief you do not hold.

Is everything confidential?

Personal conversations are treated with discretion and respect, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, including where safety or legal obligations require action. Please avoid sending sensitive medical or crisis information through the form.

Will this guarantee a transformed life?

Growth and transformation are central hopes of Christian discipleship, but everyone's journey is different. No particular spiritual, emotional, medical, financial or life outcome is guaranteed.
